Slider: drag to steer one servo (stops the wave; torque must be on). Degrees are the wave's calibrated band, 2048 counts ~ 90°. Telemetry: one bus read per servo per tick at ~50 Hz, straight on the Uno Q. Current is the servo's raw reading; its unit is not yet confirmed for the HD-1910.
